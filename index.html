Welcome

Engineering, Technology & Innovation Conference ETIC-2026

We invite researchers, academicians, engineers, industry professionals, and policymakers to ETIC-2026 — exploring the latest advancements in emerging technologies, electronics and electrical engineering.

ETIC-2026 is approved by IEEE — all accepted and presented papers will be published in IEEE Xplore, indexed in Scopus.

Since 2021 we have been instrumental in gathering technological research globally, connecting scholars across disciplines.
